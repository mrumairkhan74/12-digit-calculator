    Free 12-Digit Calculator Online

    This online desktop calculator is designed for everyday office, school and business calculations. It supports a 12-digit display, keyboard input, memory functions, percentage, tax, markup and Grand Total.

    12-digit display

    Enter numbers up to the calculator's 12-digit display limit without installing software.

    Business functions

    Use TAX+, TAX−, MU, percentage and memory keys for common business calculations.

    Keyboard support

    Use your keyboard for digits, operators, Enter, Escape, Backspace and decimal input.

    How to use the calculator

    1. Enter the first number.
    2. Select an operation such as +, −, × or ÷.
    3. Enter the second number and press =.
    4. Use M+, M−, MR and MC for memory calculations.

    Common examples

    25 + 15 = 40

    500 ÷ 4 = 125

    200 × 10 % 20

    100 × 20 MU 120 (markup)

    100 ÷ 20 MU 125 (selling price from cost & margin)

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a 12-digit calculator?

    A 12-digit calculator can display up to twelve significant numeric digits in its main display.

    Can I use my keyboard?

    Yes. Number keys and common arithmetic operators are supported, along with Enter, Escape, Backspace and the decimal key.

    Does it work on mobile?

    Yes. The calculator is responsive and designed for touch screens as well as desktop keyboards.

    How does the tax rate work?

    Enter a tax rate in the settings field (for example 15). TAX+ adds the rate; TAX− removes it from a tax-included price.
